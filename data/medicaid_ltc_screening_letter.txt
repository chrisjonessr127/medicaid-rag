Long-Term Care Medicaid Financial Screening Guidelines

Asset Limits (As of the 1st day of the application month):
- Single applicants: Less than $2,500 in countable assets.
- Married applicants: May be subject to a $32,532 asset limit.
- Countable assets include bank accounts, investments, and the cash surrender value of applicable life insurance policies.

Income Contribution:
- Monthly income must be applied toward the cost of care, less the $109 monthly personal needs allowance and any approved community, medical, or spousal allowances.

Required Documentation:
- Bank and Financial Statements: Current application month, the preceding month, and the anniversary month for each of the past five years.
- Tax Returns: Federal tax returns for the current year and the preceding four years (including all forms and schedules).
- Five-Year Asset Transfer History: Must provide type and value of asset, reason for transfer, recipient's name, and amount received. Transfers for less than fair market value may result in a penalty period.
