FutureCare Maryland Medicaid Application Verification Checklist (FC_MEDICAID CHECKLIST)

Required Documentation for LTC Medicaid Submission:
1. Identification:
- Medicare Card and Social Security Card.
- If applicant lacks Medicare: Copy of Birth Certificate and Photo ID.
- Applicants without Medicare must apply for Social Security Disability Benefits and provide the pending application receipt.

2. Health Insurance:
- Front and back copies of all health insurance cards and proof of premium payment amounts.

3. Proof of Current Benefits (Gross Monthly Amounts for both Resident and Spouse):
- Social Security Administration award letter.
- Civil Service / Office of Personnel Management (OPM).
- Veterans Administration (VA) benefits.
- Retirement, pensions, and annuity verification statements.

4. Marital Information:
- If married: Spouse's Social Security Number and Date of Birth.

5. Bank Account Statements (Checking, Savings, and Money Market):
- Statements for the month of application plus statements for the 3 full months prior to application.
- Bank transaction history printout showing balances from the date of the last statement through the current date.
- Mandatory Balance Rule: Maryland Medicaid requires proof of balance as of the 1st day of the application month.

6. Real Estate Property:
- Deed and current property tax assessment for all real property owned.

7. Shelter and Utility Costs (1 current month of expenses):
- Rent agreement, mortgage stub, homeowners insurance declaration page, property tax bill, condo fees, gas, electric, and water bills.

8. Liquid and Investment Assets:
- Current official statements for Stocks, Bonds, Certificates of Deposit (CDs), IRAs, and 401(k) accounts.

9. Life Insurance:
- Life insurance verification letter from the carrier stating both Cash Value and Face Value for all policies.

10. Burial Assets:
- Irrevocable burial trust, prepaid burial contracts, or dedicated burial accounts.

11. Vital Statistics:
- Marriage Certificate, Divorce Decree, or Death Certificate of deceased spouse.

12. Legal Authority:
- Executed Power of Attorney (POA) or Court-Ordered Legal Guardianship documents.
