Medicaid Copay Application Financial Screening Guidelines

Purpose: 
- The Medicaid application helps cover the applicant's Medicare copay. It may reduce the amount but does not necessarily cover it in full.

Asset Limits:
- A single applicant may have no more than $2,500 in liquid assets as of the first day of the month the application is submitted.
- Whole life insurance policies: Cash value is considered a countable Medicaid asset.

Conversion to Long-Term Care:
- If the applicant remains in the facility after Medicare coverage ends, the stay converts to long-term care.
- Income Contribution: The applicant's monthly income must be contributed toward the cost of care, less the $109 monthly personal needs allowance.

Five-Year Lookback:
- If assets were gifted, sold, or transferred within the past five years, additional documentation is required.
- Transfers for less than fair market value may result in a Medicaid penalty period.
