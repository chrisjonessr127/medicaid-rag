Maryland Department of Human Resources / Health and Mental Hygiene
Long-Term Care/Waiver Medical Assistance Application Checklist (DHR/FIA 9709)

Five-Year Transfer Proof:
- If property, motor vehicles, stocks, bonds, cash, or assets were sold, traded, gifted, or disposed of in the past 5 years, provide: Type of asset, reason for transfer, value of asset, who received the asset, and amount received.

Federal Tax Returns:
- Required for the current year and the preceding four years.
- If federal tax returns cannot be located, a Record of Account can be obtained from the IRS free of charge by calling 1-800-908-9946.

Bank and Financial Statements (All accounts owned and co-owned):
- Current Month (month of application).
- Previous Month (month prior to application).
- The last five years of the anniversary month of the application.

Life Insurance:
- Face and cash value of Life Insurance policies (current annual statement).
