FutureCare Cherrywood SNF Insurance Coverage Guidelines (2026)

Medicare Part A:
- Days 1-20: $0 copayment.
- Days 21-100: $217 copayment each day.
- No benefit beyond day 100. Must meet Medicare's guideline for a skillable need.

Medicare Advantage:
- Days 1-20: $0 copayment.
- Days 21-100: Varies by insurance, up to $217 copayment each day. Call plan to verify. No benefit beyond day 100.

Managed Care/Commercial:
- Subject to plan terms. May incur out-of-pocket expenses from deductibles, co-pays, or denial after the fact.

Medigap/Secondary:
- Medigap policies and secondary insurance help pay health care costs that Original Medicare doesn't cover.

VA Health Benefits:
- VA benefits and Medicare are separate. VA health won't typically cover Medicare deductibles, copayments, and coinsurance.
