Maryland Medical Assistance Program Types and Facility Admittance Guidelines

1. Long Term Care (LTC) Medicaid:
- Institutional purposes only.
- Eligibility is based upon countable liquid assets at the time of application.

2. Qualified Medicare Beneficiary (QMB):
- Helps cover copayments for hospital, Skilled Nursing Facility (SNF), and physician visits.
- Pays Medicare Part B premiums and Part D.
- Eligibility is based on income.
- Important Rule: QMB cannot be primary insurance.
- Billing / Submission Rule: QMB 257 submissions get sent directly to Tina, not Telligen.
- Restriction: Cannot admit in a Skilled Nursing Facility (SNF) under standard terms.

3. Community Medical Assistance (Community MA):
- Helps cover copayments for hospital, SNF, and physician visits; pays Part B premiums and Part D.
- Based on income (SSI recipients / income less than $350 per month).
- Can admit for less than a 30-day stay as primary insurance, provided there is an approved level / Form 3871 from the hospital or community.
- Covers Adult Day Care services.

4. MDC / BHH / Waivers (e.g., OAH, OHM, OAA):
- Refer to 257 section for biller guidance.
- Can admit for stays of less than 30 days as primary insurance with an approved level / Form 3871 from the hospital or community.
- Covers hospital, doctor, and SNF copayments.
- Offers community-based wrap-around supports: in-home care, subsidized housing / Assisted Living Facility (ALF), and Medical Day Care.

5. Specified Low-Income Medicare Beneficiary (SLMB):
- Pays for Medicare Part B premiums and Part D only.
- Does not cover copayments for hospital, SNF, or doctor visits.
- If copayment assistance is required, a full LTC application is needed.
- Requires a Short Application (Short App).

6. General Admittance and Verification Rules:
- Any medical assistance product requires a Short Application.
- Always check if the applicant receives HUD Housing, Energy Assistance, or Food Stamps (SNAP), as certain public assistance products do not appear directly on EVS (Eligibility Verification System).
- DRW (Discharge Review / Waiver): 30-day primary benefit transitions to Grace.
