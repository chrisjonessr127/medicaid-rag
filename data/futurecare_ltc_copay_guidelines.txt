Facility Guidelines: FutureCare Cherrywood (12020 Reisterstown Rd, Reisterstown, MD 21136)
Senior Patient Account Coordinator / Business Office Management Rules

Skilled Nursing Insurance Benefit and Copayment Period:
- Notice of Daily Copay: Accumulates effective for days 21 through 100 of skilled nursing facility (SNF) coverage under Medicare or Medicare Advantage.
- Secondary Insurance: Secondary insurance may assist with daily copayments; policy coverage must be reviewed prior to billing.

Discharge and Readmission Rules:
- Discharge Home: Patients well enough to safely return home may choose to discharge prior to day 21 to avoid accumulating daily out-of-pocket copay expenses. Social Workers must be consulted for discharge planning.
- The 60-Day Break in Service Rule: If a patient discharges and is later readmitted, the 100-day skilled nursing benefit will NOT restart unless there has been a full 60-day break in service.

Payment Plans:
- The facility offers structured 12-month and 18-month payment plans for balances. Arrangements are initiated through the Business Office Manager upon receipt of the first statement.

Long-Term Care Medicaid Financial Eligibility Caps:
- Single Applicants: Must have less than $2,500 in countable liquid assets as of the first day of the month for which Medicaid coverage is requested.
- Married Applicants: Must have less than $32,584 in countable liquid assets as of the first day of the month for which Medicaid coverage is requested.
- Screening: Requires a comprehensive financial screening to initiate the application.
